# Domain Context & Vocabulary

## Project: Harvest Hackathon ("De Harvester Schrijft Zijn Eigen CV")

**Harvest** is a CPION-accredited IT Post-Master Program in the Netherlands training young IT professionals ("Harvesters") and placing them with enterprise clients for 12-month assignments.

### Key Terminology & Domain Concepts
- **Harvester**: A young IT professional in the 12-month Harvest program.
- **Harvest CV Format**: The standardized, high-quality layout and content structure required by Harvest for client presentations.
- **Harvest PO (Product Owner)**: Harvest representative who evaluates, reviews, and approves/rejects Harvester CVs.
- **Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)**: Engineers working close to business goals, building reliable software ready for handoff.

### Core Objectives & Constraints
1. **Harvester CV Creation**: Allow Harvesters to generate, edit, and tailor their CV in official Harvest format.
2. **Harvest Review & Approval**: Provide Harvest with a fast, reliable verification & approval interface.
3. **Responsible AI**: Ensure AI does not invent experience/degrees (anti-hallucination sourcing), keeps costs low (< $0.05/CV), and keeps humans in the loop for final decisions.
4. **Handoff & Transparency**: Deliver live URL, source code, and complete README/Overdracht docs detailing cost and AI usage.
